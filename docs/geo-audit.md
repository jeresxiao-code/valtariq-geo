# GEO Website Audit

The V1 audit is deterministic and explainable. It fetches the public website and checks observable technical/content signals rather than claiming to know a proprietary AI ranking algorithm.

Checks include:
- descriptive title and meta description
- H1/H2 structure
- Organization JSON-LD
- Product JSON-LD
- buyer-oriented Q&A / FAQ signals
- robots.txt
- sitemap.xml
- llms.txt (experimental signal only)
- substantial crawlable text

The score is a readiness score, not a guarantee of AI citation or ranking. Findings store evidence and a concrete recommendation so reports can explain why each point was awarded.
