# GEO Action Center

The Action Center converts measured gaps into prioritized work.

Inputs:
- GEO website audit findings
- AI mention rate
- citation rate and owned-domain citations
- competitor share of voice

Recommendations remain explainable: every action records its source, observed gap, proposed action, priority, impact and effort. V1 uses deterministic rules so customers can understand why an action appears. AI-generated copy can be added later as an optional enrichment layer rather than replacing the evidence model.
